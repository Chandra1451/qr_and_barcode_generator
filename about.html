🌐 About UniversalCodeMaker.com

Engineered for Openness, Speed & Total Privacy

We built UniversalCodeMaker.com because barcode and QR code generation should be fast, standards-compliant, and fundamentally private — without predatory paywalls, tracking redirects, or subscriptions.

🔒

100% Client-Side Privacy

Every barcode and QR code is rendered directly in your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and SVG. Your sensitive Wi-Fi passwords, contact details, payment information, and serial numbers never touch a remote server or database.

⚡

Zero Paywalls or Limits

Competitors charge $15 to $30 per month for vector SVG downloads or batch label printing. At UniversalCodeMaker.com, unlimited high-resolution raster (PNG/JPEG) and scalable vector (SVG) exports are free forever for personal and commercial use.

📐

Strict Standards Accuracy

We adhere strictly to international GS1, ISO/IEC 15420, ISO/IEC 18004, and ISO/IEC 16022 specifications. Barcodes include automated Mod-10 checksum validation and proper quiet zones to guarantee high first-pass scan rates.

🎯 Our Story & Philosophy

The modern web is filled with "free" QR and barcode generator websites that bait users with initial free generation, only to hold their codes hostage behind monthly recurring paywalls or disguise dynamic redirects that can break or expire at any time. Even worse, many services log every scanned Wi-Fi key, internal SKU, and customer contact in backend databases.

UniversalCodeMaker.com was developed to solve this problem once and for all. By harnessing modern browser capabilities — client-side rendering engines, high-performance canvas manipulation, and vector SVG synthesis — we eliminate the need for server-side processing entirely.

🛠️ Open Source & Technology Stack

Our platform stands on the shoulders of giants in the open-source community. We gratefully utilize and maintain integrations with proven, battle-tested rendering engines:

bwip-js (Barcode Writer in Pure JavaScript) Powers our 1D retail and 2D industrial barcode engines with support for over 50 symbologies, strictly compliant with international GS1 and ISO standards.
qr-code-styling Enables custom-branded QR codes featuring rounded dot styles, gradient color fills, customizable eye markers, and embedded center logo branding.
jspdf (Client-Side PDF Compiler) Assembles multi-label grids on standard Avery 5160 (30-up) and Avery 5163 (10-up) sheets directly in browser memory without sending data over the network.
Vanilla ES Modules & Modern CSS Built without bloated frameworks to ensure ultra-fast sub-50ms loading times, 100/100 Core Web Vitals, and seamless performance on mobile and desktop devices.
