About UniversalCodeMaker

UniversalCodeMaker makes barcodes and QR codes in your browser. No account, no subscription, and no redirect links that stop working when a plan ends.

Who runs it

It's built and run by Primordial Paradigm. Questions, bug reports and requests for new formats: contact@primordialparadigm.com, or use the contact page.

How we check things

  • Every barcode type is read back. Before each release, about 1,500 automated tests run. Among them, every barcode the studio makes is decoded with ZXing, a separate open-source reader, and compared with what was typed.
  • Guides show their sources. Each guide ends with the standards and official pages it relies on, with the date each was last read, and shows when its facts were last checked.
  • Mistakes get fixed in the open. If something is wrong, tell us. Fixes are listed on the What changed page.

What we won't do

  • Make barcodes for ID cards, driving licences or other identity documents.
  • Make up GTIN or "barcode numbers". Retail numbers come from GS1; we only draw the barcode for a number you already have.
  • Publish statistics we can't back up.

Your codes stay in your browser

What you type into the generator is turned into a code in your browser, not sent to our server. Recent codes are kept in your browser's own storage so you can find them again. The site does use measurement and advertising services, explained in the Privacy Policy.

Free to use

There is no subscription or export charge, and you don't need to credit us. You can use the codes commercially. You remain responsible for what you encode, for using valid numbers, and for the rules of any shop or marketplace you sell through. Please read the Terms before commercial use.

Open-source parts we use

  • bwip-js draws the 9 barcode formats: EAN-13, UPC-A, ISBN, ITF-14, Code 128, Code 39, Data Matrix, Aztec and PDF417.
  • qr-code-styling draws the QR codes, including rounded dots, colours, gradients and a logo in the middle.
  • jsPDF makes the PDF label sheets (Avery 5160 and 5163) and the UPI shop poster.
  • JSZip packs batch downloads into one ZIP file.
  • zxing-wasm reads barcodes and QR codes in the scanner.
  • Everything else is plain JavaScript and CSS, with no framework.
