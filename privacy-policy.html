01

Your Codes Stay in Your Browser

Many online generators send what you type to their server, make the image there and send it back. UniversalCodeMaker doesn't. Every barcode and QR code, check digit, style and download (PNG, SVG, EPS, PDF and ZIP) is made by JavaScript running in your own browser.

Nothing Sent to a Server
What you type, the logos you add and the lists you paste are never sent to our web server, to a database, to Google Analytics or to advertisers.
Recent Codes, on Your Device Only
The studio remembers your 6 most recent barcodes and website-link QR codes in this browser so you can reopen them. Wi-Fi, contact, payment and other QR codes are never saved. The list never leaves your device; press the × next to “Recents” to delete it.

Barcode & QR Scanner and camera access. Our Scanner reads codes from images you choose, drop or paste, or from your device camera. The image or camera frame is decoded inside your browser by a self-hosted, open-source reader (ZXing-C++ compiled to WebAssembly); it is never uploaded, stored or shared, and the decoded text is not sent to us, to analytics or to advertising partners. The camera is used only after you tap Use camera and grant your browser’s permission; it is switched off as soon as a code is found, when you press Stop, or when you leave or hide the page. No audio is ever requested. Other pages of this site are not allowed to use the camera. You can withdraw camera permission at any time in your browser’s site settings.

Analytics, Advertising & Cookies

Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages and tools are used. When it is allowed (see section 4), it sets two cookies, _ga and _ga_<ID>, which by default last 2 years, and sends Google information such as the pages you view, your browser, device type and approximate location. Google states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. If a link puts code content in the page address (?data= or ?payload=), our script removes it before Analytics sees the address.

Google AdSense. The site is free and is meant to be supported by ads served by Google AdSense.

GS1 Commercial Barcode Notice & Legal Disclaimer

Mandatory Commercial Barcode Notice (EAN-13, UPC-A, ITF-14)
This application generates mathematically valid barcode graphics based on standard open international symbology specifications (ISO/IEC 15420, ISO/IEC 15417, and GS1 General Specifications).

However, generating an EAN or UPC barcode graphic through this free utility DOES NOT grant legal ownership, product licensing, or international authorization to assign that number to commercial retail products.

Legitimate Global Trade Item Numbers (GTINs), company prefixes, and retail barcodes must be officially licensed through GS1 (GS1.org) or an authorized international barcode registry. Using unassigned or duplicate numbers on retail products may result in immediate rejection by retailers, point-of-sale collisions, and commercial liability.

Pre-Print Verification & Quality Notice

Barcode readability is influenced by several external factors beyond digital graphic generation, including printer DPI resolution, thermal head wear, substrate/paper finish, contrast ratio, ink bleed, and scanning hardware optics.

Pre-Flight Test Proof Requirement
Always produce a physical test proof and verify scanning with an actual handheld barcode scanner or smartphone camera prior to mass printing labels, packaging, or marketing collateral.

Universal Code Studio, its authors, and contributors assume no liability for misprinted packaging, failed retail scans, or manufacturing delays resulting from unverified barcode artwork.
