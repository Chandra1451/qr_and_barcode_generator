01

Zero-Data Retention Architecture

Traditional web generators require you to upload your sensitive payloads—such as private Wi-Fi passwords, contact card vCards, cryptocurrency addresses, or proprietary inventory SKUs—to a remote server where barcodes are rendered and returned as images. This architecture poses serious confidentiality and regulatory risks.

Universal Code Studio operates on a strictly client-side paradigm. Every barcode algorithm, checksum computation (GS1 Modulo 10, Luhn Modulo 10, Code 128 Modulo 103), QR styling engine, and vector SVG/PDF exporter executes exclusively inside your device's web browser memory (RAM) using pure JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas.

Zero Server-Side Transmission
Your keystrokes, URLs, credentials, and uploaded logo files are never transmitted to our web servers or any cloud database.
Ephemeral Browser Memory
When you close or refresh your browser tab, all entered payloads and generated graphics are instantly purged from your device memory.

India: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) & IT Act

For visitors, businesses, and developers residing in India, Universal Code Studio operates in strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act 2023) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 (including the Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information Rules, 2011):

Data Principal Protections
Under the DPDP Act 2023, you are the Data Principal. Because all barcode payloads are processed strictly inside your device's browser RAM, no personal data is collected or retained by us as a Data Fiduciary.
Sensitive Indian Identifiers
Inputs containing UPI payment strings, phone numbers, contact vCards, or PAN/GSTIN numbers never leave your local physical device and are never transmitted to our servers.

Key Compliance Commitments under the DPDP Act, 2023:

  • Notice & Lawful Grounds (Sections 5 & 6): This policy serves as the statutory notice informing you that code generation is executed client-side without collecting digital personal data. Non-essential advertising cookies (Google AdSense) are deployed only in accordance with informed consent standards.
  • Right to Access & Information (Section 11): As a Data Principal, you have the right to obtain confirmation of whether digital personal data is processed. Because we maintain a zero-retention architecture, we hold no personal data records linked to you.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12): In-memory barcode data is instantly erased the moment your browser tab or session is terminated.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13): You have the right to readily accessible grievance redressal provided by our designated Grievance Officer (see Section 11 for contact details).
  • Right to Nominate (Section 14): You have the right to nominate an individual to exercise your data rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Protection of Children (Section 9): We do not track, profile, or undertake behavioral monitoring of children, nor do we serve targeted advertising directed towards minors.
Information Technology Act, 2000 (SPDI Rules)
Universal Code Studio implements comprehensive reasonable security practices and procedures pursuant to Section 43A of the IT Act, 2000 and Rule 8 of the SPDI Rules, 2011, ensuring complete protection of client-side operations against unauthorized access, loss, or tampering.

GS1 Commercial Barcode Notice & Legal Disclaimer

Mandatory Commercial Barcode Notice (EAN-13, UPC-A, ITF-14)
This application generates mathematically valid barcode graphics based on standard open international symbology specifications (ISO/IEC 15420, ISO/IEC 15417, and GS1 General Specifications).

However, generating an EAN or UPC barcode graphic through this free utility DOES NOT grant legal ownership, product licensing, or international authorization to assign that number to commercial retail products.

Legitimate Global Trade Item Numbers (GTINs), company prefixes, and retail barcodes must be officially licensed through GS1 (GS1.org) or an authorized international barcode registry. Using unassigned or duplicate numbers on retail products may result in immediate rejection by retailers, point-of-sale collisions, and commercial liability.

Pre-Print Verification & Quality Notice

Barcode readability is influenced by several external factors beyond digital graphic generation, including printer DPI resolution, thermal head wear, substrate/paper finish, contrast ratio, ink bleed, and scanning hardware optics.

Pre-Flight Test Proof Requirement
Always produce a physical test proof and verify scanning with an actual handheld barcode scanner or smartphone camera prior to mass printing labels, packaging, or marketing collateral.

Universal Code Studio, its authors, and contributors assume no liability for misprinted packaging, failed retail scans, or manufacturing delays resulting from unverified barcode artwork.
