🧭 What Code Do I Need for My Product? (Customer Decision Guide)

Choosing the right barcode format depends on where your product is sold, the packaging substrate, and scanner infrastructure:

👉 Packing master cartons containing multiple retail units?

ITF-14 is the global standard barcode printed on outer shipping cases containing 6, 12, or 24 individual retail products.

👉 Can ITF-14 be scanned at point-of-sale retail checkouts?

No. ITF-14 is strictly for non-retail logistics, wholesale distribution, and warehouse cross-docking.

👉 How does ITF-14 link to the retail item inside?

The 14-digit number incorporates an Packaging Indicator digit (1–8) followed by the 12-digit GTIN of the enclosed retail unit and a final check digit.

⚖️ Legal & Compliance Guide

ITF-14 Printing Standards & Bearer Bar Guidelines

  • ⚠ Always include bearer bars (minimum 4.8mm thick) when printing directly onto corrugated cartons to prevent uneven flexographic plate impression.
  • ⚠ Ensure the barcode is placed at least 32mm from the bottom edge of the box and at least 19mm from vertical edges to prevent corner folding distortion.
  • ⚠ The final 14th digit is a mandatory GS1 Modulo-10 checksum calculated across the first 13 digits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does an ITF-14 barcode have thick bearer bars? ▼

Bearer bars equalize printing plate pressure when printing directly onto rough, undulating corrugated cardboard, and prevent barcode laser scanners from misreading a partial slice of the code.

Can an ITF-14 barcode be scanned at retail cash registers? ▼

No. ITF-14 barcodes are strictly meant for master cases and wholesale shipping cartons, not consumer-facing retail checkouts.
