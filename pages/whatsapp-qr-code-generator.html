Common questions

How do I write the number? ▼

Use the full international number without the + or leading zeros, for example 919876543210 for an Indian mobile or 15551234567 for a US one. The generator removes spaces and symbols.

Does the message send automatically? ▼

No. The chat opens with the greeting in the text box, and the person decides whether to send it.

What happens on a computer or a phone without WhatsApp? ▼

The wa.me link opens a WhatsApp page in the browser, which offers WhatsApp Web or the app download.

Does the generator keep my number? ▼

No. The code is built in your browser, and the generator does not upload the number or message.
