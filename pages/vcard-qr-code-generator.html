How to make, print and test a vCard QR code

Step by step

  1. Open the studio with the vCard QR type selected (the button above does this).
  2. Fill in the fields you want to share: first and last name, company, job title, phone, email, website and address. Leave out anything you don't want on the card.
  3. Write the phone number in international format with the country code, for example +1 555 019 2834 or +44 20 7946 0000.
  4. Scan the preview with your phone and check that Add to Contacts shows every field correctly.
  5. Download SVG for business cards and print design, or PNG for email signatures and slides.

Printing & size

  • Business cards: a standard card is 85 × 55 mm (3.5 × 2 in). Print the code at least 2.5 cm (1 in) wide, with a clear white margin around it.
  • Fewer fields scan faster. A vCard is plain text inside the code, so every extra field makes the pattern denser. Name, phone, email and website are usually enough.
  • Contrast: keep the code dark on a light background, even if your card design is dark. Put the code on a white panel.
  • Logo: if you add a logo in the centre, the studio switches to the highest error correction level automatically. Keep the logo small and test the final print.

Common mistakes

  • Phone numbers without the country code. They may dial the wrong number, or none, when scanned abroad.
  • Testing on screen only. Always scan the printed proof at its final size, especially on small cards.
  • Expecting to edit it later. The contact details are stored in the code itself, so a new job title or phone number needs a new code.
  • Adding a photo. Photos are far too large for a QR code. Share them through your website or LinkedIn link instead.

Is a vCard QR code the right fit?

Printed business cards?

Yes. Put the code on the back with a white margin around it, at least 2.5 cm wide.

Your details change often?

A vCard code can't be changed after printing. A Website URL code that points to your own contact page can stay the same while you update the page.

Want to share a photo or portfolio?

Add your website or LinkedIn address to the card. Photos don't fit in a QR code.

Rules to know

Before you print

  • Only include details you are happy for anyone holding the card to save.
  • Scan the printed proof at its final size on an iPhone and an Android phone.

Common questions

What does the other person see when they scan it? ▼

Their camera shows a contact preview. Tapping it opens the contacts app with your details filled in, and they decide whether to save it.

Can I add two phone numbers? ▼

Not at the moment. There is one phone field, saved as a work number, so use the number you most want people to call.

Does the generator keep my details? ▼

No. The card is built in your browser, and the generator does not upload your contact details.

Will a vCard QR code work on both iPhone and Android? ▼

Yes. The iPhone Camera app and Android camera or Google Lens recognise vCard codes and offer to add the contact. We write vCard 3.0, which both read.

Can I change the details after printing? ▼

No. The details are stored inside the code, so there is no server or subscription behind it, and also no way to edit it. If something changes, generate a new code.

Can I put my photo or logo in the contact card? ▼

A photo can't fit inside a QR code. You can place a small logo in the centre of the code itself as decoration; it isn't added to the saved contact.
