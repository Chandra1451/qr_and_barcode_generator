UPC vs EAN-13: Which Barcode Do You Need?

UPC-A (12 digits) and EAN-13 (13 digits) are the primary barcodes used at retail checkout around the world. They share the same physical size, bar geometry and scanner compatibility. The difference lies in where your product number originates, how quiet zones are arranged, and where you sell.

The short answer

Quick rule of thumb:
  • Selling primarily in the US or Canada? Use a 12-digit UPC-A.
  • Selling outside North America, or exporting globally? Use a 13-digit EAN-13.
  • Selling on Amazon? Amazon accepts both UPC and EAN product identifiers. Use whichever format your GS1 record issued.
  • Both scan at checkout worldwide. Modern point-of-sale scanners read both symbologies. North American retailers adopted EAN-13 scanning readiness under the 2005 "Sunrise" initiative, and GS1 US confirms that EAN codes work in the US while UPC codes work in Europe.
  • Use the format your number was issued in. If your GS1 certificate assigns a 12-digit number (GTIN-12), encode it in UPC-A. If it assigns a 13-digit number (GTIN-13), encode it in EAN-13. Do not invent digits or buy separate numbers for different countries.
  • International sellers exporting to North America: If your domestic product carries a valid GS1-issued EAN-13, most US retailers and marketplaces accept it without relabelling. Confirm with your distributor or buyer whether they require a 12-digit UPC-A for legacy inventory systems.

Can I use EAN-13 in the US, or UPC-A outside it?

Yes, in both directions. Before 2005, products sold in the US and Canada had to carry a 12-digit UPC, so foreign makers relabelled their goods. GS1 US then set 1 January 2005 (the "Sunrise" date) as the day retailers and their partners that scan UPCs should handle 13-digit EAN-13 codes too.

  • GS1 Greece: "Since that date most retailers have been able to handle products marked with EAN-13 symbols."
  • GS1 US guidance confirms: "your EAN code works in the U.S. and … your UPC is functional in Europe."
  • Amazon accepts UPC, EAN, JAN and ISBN product numbers per Amazon GTIN requirements (see also our guide: Do I need a barcode to sell on Amazon?).

One caution from GS1 Greece: if you're exporting to the US or Canada, check with your buyer. If a retailer insists on a 12-digit UPC, your GS1 office can arrange a UPC company prefix.

Converting between UPC-A and EAN-13

Because point-of-sale systems operate internationally, modern retail databases store product identifiers in standardized 13-digit (GTIN-13) or 14-digit (GTIN-14) data fields. Here is how numbers convert mathematically:

  • UPC-A to EAN-13 (Add a leading 0): To represent a 12-digit UPC-A in a 13-digit EAN system, place a single 0 at the beginning. The check digit does not change.
    Example: UPC-A 036000291452 becomes EAN-13 0036000291452.
    Why? In the GS1 Modulo-10 checksum calculation, the added 0 adds 0 to the weighted sum. The final check digit remains identical.
  • EAN-13 to UPC-A (Only if starting with 0): An EAN-13 can only be converted to a UPC-A if its first digit is 0. If an EAN-13 begins with any other digit (such as 590... or 890...), it represents an international GS1 prefix and cannot be shortened to a 12-digit UPC. Do not truncate the first digit to force a foreign EAN into a UPC barcode.
  • 14-Digit Database Padding (GTIN-14): In warehouse logistics and EDI systems, both codes are padded with leading zeros to fill 14 characters:
    UPC-A: 00036000291452
    EAN-13: 005901234123457

Why cheap reseller barcodes fail on Amazon and retail:

Marketplaces such as Amazon automatically verify barcode numbers against the official GS1 database (via Verified by GS1, formerly GEPIR) under Amazon's Product ID policy. When a reseller sells you a recycled UPC, the database lists the original licensee—not your brand. If the company name does not match, Amazon flags the listing as invalid and can suspend or remove the product.

Smaller and related barcodes

  • EAN-8 and UPC-E: short versions for very small packs (8 digits). UniversalCodeMaker does not generate these.
  • ISBN: books use an EAN-13 whose number starts with 978 or 979, from your national ISBN agency rather than GS1. ISBN barcode generator.
  • ITF-14: outer cartons and cases use a 14-digit number in an ITF-14 barcode, not EAN-13 or UPC-A. ITF-14 generator.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Buying recycled barcodes from brokers. GS1 US warns that numbers associated with another company risk rejection, and Amazon removes listings whose UPCs don't match GS1's database.
  • Buying separate UPC and EAN numbers for the same item. Unnecessary expense: scanners worldwide read both; an EAN-13 scans in the US, and a UPC-A scans in Europe.
  • Truncating the first digit of an EAN-13. Only EAN-13s starting with 0 have a UPC-A counterpart. International prefixes 1–9 cannot be shortened.
  • Encroaching on quiet zones. UPC-A needs 9X margins each side; EAN-13 needs 11X on the left. Placing artwork, text or box edges too close causes register read errors.
  • Confusing SKU with UPC or EAN. A SKU is your internal warehouse inventory code. A GTIN is the universal retail identifier recognized across the supply chain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a UPC the same as an EAN? ▼

They are part of the same GS1 barcode family. A UPC-A carries a 12-digit number (GTIN-12) primarily used in North America, while an EAN-13 carries a 13-digit number (GTIN-13) used worldwide. Adding a leading 0 to a UPC-A converts it into a valid 13-digit GTIN without changing the check digit.

Can I use an EAN-13 barcode in the United States? ▼

Yes. Since the 2005 Sunrise date, point-of-sale barcode scanners across North America have been equipped to decode 13-digit EAN-13 symbols alongside 12-digit UPCs. If supplying a US retailer, confirm whether their inventory system accepts GTIN-13 or requires GTIN-12.

What is the cheapest way to get a legitimate UPC or EAN barcode? ▼

For small businesses needing 1 to 9 codes, GS1 US offers single GTIN-12 identifiers for $30 with no annual renewal fees. In the UK, GS1 UK offers small business memberships for £50 + VAT per year. Avoid cheap third-party brokers, as unverified reseller barcodes risk rejection on Amazon and retail stores. For a complete breakdown of licensing options, see our guide: How to get a barcode for your product.

How do I convert a UPC-A to an EAN-13? ▼

Add a 0 in front of the 12 digits. The check digit does not change because multiplying the added 0 by 3 adds nothing to the checksum calculation. For example, UPC-A 036000291452 becomes EAN-13 0036000291452.

Are UPC-A and EAN-13 barcodes the same physical size? ▼

Yes. At the nominal 100% scale, both barcodes share the identical overall width of 37.29 mm (including blank margins) and bar height of 22.85 mm at X = 0.330 mm, per GS1 General Specifications.

Do I need both a UPC and an EAN for my product? ▼

No. One universal GS1 product identifier functions globally. Scanners in Europe can read UPC-A barcodes, and modern scanners in the US can read EAN-13 barcodes. Marketplaces such as Amazon accept both formats.
