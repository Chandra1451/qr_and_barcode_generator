Frequently Asked Support Questions

Can you restore a QR code or barcode I generated earlier? ▼

No. Because UniversalCodeMaker.com is 100% client-side, your data never reaches our servers. Once you close your browser tab, your codes exist only in the files you downloaded to your computer or device.

Can I request support for a custom barcode symbology or 2D standard? ▼

Yes! We actively expand our catalog based on user demand. Select "New Barcode Symbology Request" above with the standard name (e.g., Code 39, Codabar, GS1-128) and specifications.

Are generated codes really free for commercial packaging? ▼

Yes, 100% free with no royalties or subscriptions. You own full rights to your generated graphics. Please see our Terms of Service for complete details.
